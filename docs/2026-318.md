# Update for November 14, 2026

## 09:40 MST

### Starting _Vantage_

Decided to participate in the 2026 NASA Space Apps challenge, the Lunar Landing
Site Navigator seems a interesting one. It seems to be a navigational interface
problem. The data is all out there, but the tools that show it are built for
experts and make comparing sites and dates slow. That gap is exactly the kind of
thing I love working on.

### The name

I went back and forth on the name more than I'd like to admit. I tried a few
short ones like _V1_ and _V01_, but they either carried other meanings or looked
like a beta version. _Vantage_ just felt right. It's a position with a clear
view of what's around it, and it's about choosing the best path before
committing.

### More than light and Earth

The challenge is about sunlight and Earth visibility at the south pole, but I
don't want to build just a lighting calculator. The way I see it, a planner is
picking a place and a time against conditions that keep changing.

Lunar landing conditions are the Sun for power and Earth for a link home.
Tomorrow they could be temperature or slope. So I'm building it as a conditions
navigator, with this challenge as the first case.

The data should come first. The main screen is a simple grid of sites against
dates, with a clear answer in each cell and the reason behind it. There will be
a globe, but only to help with orientation.
